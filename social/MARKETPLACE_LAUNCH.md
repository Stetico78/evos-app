# EVOS Marketplace Launch

## Objective
Activate two acquisition loops at the same time:
1. Clients -> `/marketplace` -> service -> verified professional -> reservation.
2. Professionals -> `/professionals` -> application -> verification -> published services -> first client.

Do not scale paid advertising until the full production funnel is measurable and at least one real booking conversion is validated.

## Core message for clients
**No busques más opciones. Encuentra tu siguiente paso.**

EVOS connects personal evolution, verified professionals and a booking system in one place. Start with what you need today: body, mind, emotions, relationships, fitness, massage, tarot, astrology or wellness.

Primary CTA: **VER SERVICIOS EVOS** -> `/marketplace`

## Core message for professionals
**Consigue clientes. EVOS organiza la conexión.**

Join the EVOS professional network, publish verified services and receive reservations. Launch plan: no monthly fee, 20% only on the first EVOS-acquired client connection and 0% marketplace commission on repeat bookings.

Primary CTA: **QUIERO ENTRAR COMO PROFESIONAL** -> `/professionals`

## Channel format
### Instagram / Facebook
- Reel 1: problem -> "Tienes demasiadas opciones y ninguna dirección" -> EVOS -> marketplace CTA.
- Reel 2: show service categories quickly -> "Elige qué quieres trabajar hoy" -> CTA.
- Reel 3 professionals: "¿Eres entrenador, coach, masajista o profesional del bienestar?" -> professional CTA.
- Stories: poll by need (cuerpo / mente / emociones / bienestar) + link sticker.
- Carousel: 1 problem, 2 EVOS guide, 3 verified professional, 4 booking, 5 follow-up.

### TikTok
- 15-25 s vertical hooks.
- One problem per video; no platform explanation longer than one sentence.
- End with one CTA only: Marketplace or Professional Network.

### LinkedIn
- Professional recruitment and partnership focus.
- Explain verified network, transparent commission and recurring-client economics.
- Target trainers, coaches, wellness professionals, studios and independent service providers.

### WhatsApp
Client message:
> Ya puedes elegir tu siguiente paso en EVOS: servicios, profesional, precio y reserva en un solo recorrido. Entra aquí: [EVOS Marketplace]

Professional message:
> Estamos abriendo la red profesional EVOS. Perfil + servicios + reservas + captación. La entrada Launch no tiene cuota mensual: EVOS cobra solo cuando aporta un cliente nuevo. Solicitud: [EVOS Professionals]

### Google Business / local communities
- Publish service-specific posts rather than generic EVOS branding.
- Use city + service + real availability when local provider capacity is confirmed.

## 7-day organic launch sequence
Day 1: EVOS Marketplace reveal + client CTA.
Day 2: Tarot / astrology / carta service spotlight.
Day 3: Massage / body / fitness service spotlight.
Day 4: Mind / emotional / relationships / meditation spotlight with non-clinical wording unless licensed provider is verified.
Day 5: Professional recruitment campaign.
Day 6: How booking works + availability + verification trust.
Day 7: Results / first real booking / professional onboarding evidence; only use real metrics.

## Conversion tracking
Use UTM links per channel and campaign. Minimum events:
- marketplace_view
- service_view
- reserve_started
- booking_created
- professional_page_view
- professional_application
- professional_verified
- first_marketplace_booking
- repeat_booking

Track conversion separately for client acquisition and professional acquisition.

## Paid promotion gate
Only start paid traffic after production validation proves:
1. Login works.
2. Marketplace loads.
3. Professional availability is visible.
4. Reservation succeeds.
5. Commission snapshot is correct.
6. Conversion events are measurable.

Then test small controlled campaigns by service/category. Do not buy broad "EVOS awareness" traffic before a measurable booking funnel exists.

## Safety / trust rules
- Never claim medical, psychological or clinical treatment from non-licensed providers.
- Require verification before a professional is public.
- Show price, duration, modality and cancellation conditions before payment.
- Reviews must come only from completed bookings.
- Do not publish invented testimonials, availability, results or client counts.
